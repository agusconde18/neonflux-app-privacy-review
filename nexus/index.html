NeonFlux Labs privacy policies

Privacy policy for NeonFlux Labs utility apps

Effective date: October 16, 2026. Publisher: NeonFlux Labs. Contact: argentina.developers@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Android applications listed below. Each is a separate app with its own local records. There is no account, developer-operated cloud database, or cross-app record synchronisation.

Records on your device

Entries, notes, amounts, checklist items, timer settings and saved decisions are stored in the app’s private local storage. These records are not uploaded to NeonFlux Labs or supplied by the app to its advertising service. Android backup and device transfer of these app records are excluded. This is not an end-to-end encrypted cloud service.

You can remove these records and scheduled reminders through Privacy & local data → Clear local data, Android’s app-storage controls, or uninstalling the app. Clearing local data does not delete images selected from a separate file provider or data held by Google advertising services. Records remain locally until you remove them; NeonFlux Labs has no server copy to recover or delete.

Images, location and reminders

Screenshot Cleaner accesses only images you explicitly select in Android’s file picker. It does not request whole-library access or upload selected images. It requests confirmation before asking the selected provider to delete an original. A provider can refuse deletion or synchronise a deletion to its own cloud service; that provider’s policy also applies.

ParkPin & Meter optionally requests approximate location permission to save a recent approximate position locally. You can instead name a spot manually. The app does not pass saved coordinates to its advertising SDK. Choosing directions sends the selected spot to the external maps app you choose; that app handles the request under its own policy.

Apps with deadline or timer features can request notification permission after you save a reminder. Android handles local inexact alarms; battery restrictions, notification choices and device state may delay or prevent alerts. No notification is sent to another person.

Advertising and consent services

These apps integrate Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Advertising requests start only when the consent service permits them. Local core features remain available when ads are unavailable. Privacy choices appear when required by Google’s consent service; the availability and wording of these choices depend on your region and Google’s configuration.

The Google advertising SDK can collect and share the device IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), ad and product interactions, diagnostic/performance information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising data is transmitted using TLS. These advertising disclosures are separate from the local task records described above.

Use the app’s advertising privacy choices when offered and Android’s advertising-ID/privacy controls to manage available options. Google’s data handling and retention are described in Google’s privacy policy and advertising information. Resetting an app’s local records does not constitute deletion of Google-held advertising data. NeonFlux Labs does not offer a promise to erase data it does not control.

Sharing you choose

Some apps offer a share action or directions. No transfer of your saved task content is made through these actions until you choose them and select a destination. External apps, image providers and websites then operate under their own privacy practices. The apps do not read your messaging accounts or send messages to your contacts.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or local data controls, contact argentina.developers@gmail.com. If you email support, the email and information you choose to send are used to answer your request; please do not send private notes, passwords or unrelated personal records. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

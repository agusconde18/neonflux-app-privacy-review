AsterVela privacy policy

Updated 2026-10-16 · version 0.2.9

Publisher and contact

AsterVela is published by NeonFlux Labs. For support or privacy questions, contact argentina.developers@gmail.com. The current policy is available at https://agusconde18.github.io/neonflux-app-privacy-review/astervela/.

What the app uses

If you create a birth profile, the app uses its date, local time when supplied, time certainty, chosen place name, coordinates, IANA zone, place source and any explicit UTC-offset choice to calculate your chart. The saved place is preserved independently of catalog updates. Notes, their creation times, and language/theme settings are also stored locally. A clearly labeled sample chart works without a profile or account. City searches and unsaved coordinate fields stay in memory; this build requests no location permission and does not obtain your GPS location.

Where data goes

Calculations and storage happen on this device. This build has no Internet permission, account, advertising, analytics, remote server, or real purchase flow. It does not send your birth profile or notes to the developer, third parties, analytics or application logs.

Storage and protection

Saved profiles, notes and settings are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key in Android Keystore and an app-private file. Hardware protection depends on the device; it is not guaranteed. Android backup and device-transfer exclusions are configured. Your operating system, keyboard and actions such as screenshots are outside this app's storage controls. An unlocked device can display your data.

Retention and deletion

Saved data remains until you delete it. Delete an individual note in Journal, or use Settings > Erase all local data to remove the saved profile, all notes, settings and encryption key, and clear unsaved drafts. Uninstalling removes app-private data. There is no account or server copy to delete, and no recovery or synchronization service. Deletion is not a guarantee of forensic overwriting of device storage.

Unsaved drafts and errors

Unsaved profile edits and notes stay in memory while you navigate or recreate the screen. They are lost when Android ends the app process. If saved data cannot be opened, the app preserves the encrypted file and blocks changes until you retry or explicitly erase it.

Purpose and changes

Astronomical calculations describe positions. Original interpretations are symbolic entertainment and reflection, not predictive truth, therapy or financial guidance. If future versions introduce online services or purchases, the policy and store disclosures must be reviewed before those features are enabled.
